## Integrated execution

The existing authenticated scheduler endpoint has explicit monthly and weekly dispatch. Monthly skips weekly/paused projects before lease admission and summary email. A proposed database cron selects up to twenty weekly projects fairly every five minutes, skips active leases and isolates failures; it never switches a project or funds a budget. Weekly uses the existing shared lease/coordinator, project-local preparation time, current/next-week recovery, and exact publication instants. At most one paid research/content/image stage is admitted per project per visit. Direct native OpenAI admission, result allowance and provider expense/permit gates remain separate and mandatory.

Research is retained with a pinned owner opportunity or explicitly labelled generated hypothesis, source observation provenance, accepted knowledge references, fact dependencies and immutable context hashes. Generation rechecks the selected context. Content and required image use server-allocated once-only request/output IDs and independent archives. Retained stages resume without provider replay. Delivery evidence is recorded in the same transaction as workspace insertion; deletion stays distinguishable from incomplete delivery. Owner edits, changed knowledge, cancellation, uncertain research and lost acknowledgements hold instead of authorizing paid replays or restoring owner-deleted output. Proposed generated visuals remain private and require review; no synthetic score approves pixels.

Exact automatic publication now rederives the saved version and blockers, rechecks current explicit autopilot authority, and commits approval plus queue insertion under the same workspace revision/lease lock. It cannot revive withdrawn approval or queue competing dates. Weekly owner-reviewed completed content requires an existing exact approval before automatic queue admission. Existing connector final source/entitlement/transport and destination checks remain authoritative.

Four-language weekly UI shows actual durable research/content/image stages, owner-edit holds, cancellation and deduplicated in-app progress. Cancellation preserves drafts/archives and reserves the slot; it refuses active workers or an active publication queue. Expired uncertain content/image can reconcile only against the exact independent archive; unknown research needs explicit cancellation and never reruns automatically.

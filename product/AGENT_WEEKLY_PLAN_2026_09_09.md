## Decisions from the owner conversation

- Milo coordinates specialists with distinct responsibilities and visibly AI faces, learning each project's business, brand and niche to create and improve its content.
- Invite a brand-guidelines upload during setup. Offer website-led setup and a skip path so businesses without a brand book can proceed. PDF and DOCX are proposed first formats, pending parsing/visual acceptance.
- Prepare the coming week's selected publication slots before that week starts, using refreshed website and catalog information. Keep publication days, times and timezone configurable. Two articles a week is an example, not a fixed package allowance.
- Detect new/removed products, specification changes, prices and offers before research. Recheck relevant facts before each publication because changes can happen after preparation.
- Improve through saved project knowledge, explicit owner corrections and measured results. Each project's private knowledge remains separate; portfolio access does not authorize reuse of another client's material.
- Native text and image generation stays on direct OpenAI, with no Lovable AI-credit dependency or fallback. Hosting and deployment are separate services. Model choice and actual cost/quality still require the existing controlled benchmark.

The owner's 17 screenshots describe seven SEO workflows. They are reference checklists, not runtime instructions or evidence that their suggested outputs are already available. Search demand, competitor gaps, topic structure, content creation, AI-answer readiness, local/entity consistency and growth measurement all remain in scope. Neither a prompt nor an agent face establishes live data access, expertise or ranking performance.

### Backlinks & Authority specialist — owner follow-up

It shares the project’s brand, niche, audience and content inventory, with its own role/face and the same bounded execution, permissions and learning controls. The specialist researches relevant websites and competitor referring-domain gaps, assesses opportunities using available relevance/quality evidence rather than one score alone, and monitors observed new/lost links. It coordinates with Search and Content to propose useful assets worth referencing, then prepares outreach drafts or supplier-placement proposals tied to a target page, rationale, available price and budget. Separate research/proposals from sending outreach or placing orders. Acceptance includes project-specific opportunity evidence, new/lost observations with dates, authorized-only send/order behavior, duplicate/uncertain-order recovery, placement verification and bounded expense. ## Weekly operating cycle

Example: publish Tuesday and Thursday at the selected local time; prepare both on the previous Friday, leaving review time before Monday. Preparation day, cutoff and review lead time are configurable. Setup midweek shows a realistic first preparation window and uncovered slots. Keep monthly strategy and commercial quotas separate from weekly preparation.

1. **Refresh business information.** Load the authorized schedule, owner-pinned campaigns, existing work and budget. Fetch bounded website/catalog changes and compare against the last successful observations. An inaccessible page is unknown, not proof of deletion. Track source coverage and last checked time.
2. **Research using current knowledge.** Prioritize audience needs, offers, evidence and business value. Reuse existing pages and suitable drafts; avoid duplicate intent and competing pages. A new product informs research without automatically displacing owner-pinned topics.
3. **Prepare the week's slots.** Research missing topics, draft or revise the needed content, prepare visuals when required, check brand/evidence/links, and save durable results. Show each slot as missing, drafted, awaiting review, ready or actually queued. A saved draft alone does not mean a publication is queued.
4. **Notify appropriately.** Summarize website changes, prepared work and specific decisions once in-app, with optional digest delivery under existing recipient/channel settings. Respect pause, quiet hours and deduplication. Report partial coverage honestly.
5. **Recheck before publication.** Check the facts each article depends on: relevant product specifications, variant/market/currency, price, availability, active offer and links. Evaluate offer expiry at the scheduled publication time. Record the freshness cutoff; a check cannot guarantee a source never changes afterward.
6. **Resolve changes within authority.** Revise only affected content, validate again and bind permission to the changed asset version. Review mode needs approval of that version. Autopilot follows its configured action/project authority. Unknown critical facts or missing approval hold the article with a clear reason; no invented facts, silent date moves or unbounded retries.
7. **Verify and learn.** Verify the destination after publication and connect it to the action, sources and published revision. Measure outcomes later over comparable windows. Flag affected existing published blogs and propose updates under their edit/publish permissions.

For connected stores, proposed authenticated product/inventory events enqueue a bounded recheck of current source state. Deduplicate and tolerate delayed/out-of-order/missing events; keep periodic reconciliation. Public-site-only projects use bounded scheduled checks and disclose coverage limitations. Do not present proposed webhook ingestion as an existing connector capability.

Migrate the legacy monthly generator and weekly coordinator together so only one owns a publication slot. Preserve existing booked articles, owner edits, approvals, stable slot/output IDs, pauses and cancellations. Reuse leases and recovery; on rollback pause the new coordinator and reconcile outstanding jobs before restoring a single scheduler. Never run both generators over the same slots. Handle timezone/DST, month boundaries, late approvals and incomplete weeks explicitly.

## Learning and improvement

- **Project memory:** an explicit instruction such as “remember this tone for this project” saves a scoped, versioned and reversible preference. Routine copy edits stay local unless the owner establishes a recurring rule. Ambiguous or conflicting lessons become proposals.
- **Factual learning:** dated source evidence supports a claim only to its actual extent. Owner approval of style and an accepted AI-generated article are not independent factual evidence. Prevent generated content from validating its own unsupported additions.
- **Outcome learning:** connect changes to observed clicks/leads/conversions where available. Missing data is not zero. Low-volume results and simple before/after comparisons remain tentative; weekly preparation does not imply measurable SEO impact within a week.
- **Workflow improvement:** compare a proposed prompt/tool sequence against the current version on fixed briefs, including quality, regressions and total cost. Release versioned improvements through development review with rollback. Agents cannot raise their permissions, budgets or publication authority to improve their own score.

Start with persistent knowledge and selective retrieval. Model retraining is not required for remembering a changed price or an approved wording preference. Shared general methods may improve through reviewed evaluations; private client material is not pooled by default.

## Economics and delivery limits

Bound each job's input/context, source fetches, tools, duration, retries and maximum provider expense. Retrieve the relevant project records and reuse unchanged summaries/research. Refresh/revoke caches when their source or permission changes. A single price change must not regenerate every article or invoke the entire team.

Measure cost per accepted deliverable including initial document parsing, visual interpretation, indexing/storage, refreshes, research, writing, review, revisions, images and uncertain provider outcomes. Model active projects and publication slots, not registered users alone. Decide included maintenance/revision allowances in D01; no fixed price or unlimited monitoring promise follows from this plan.

Keep provider expense, customer result allowances and publishing entitlement distinct. Retain completed work when budgets run out. A job retry/recovery must not charge for the same completed generation again. Already prepared content remains publishable when only generation quota is exhausted, subject to required freshness checks and valid publishing permission. If a necessary paid check cannot be admitted, show that specific blocker rather than claim freshness.

The existing USD5 authorization is for exactly one scan, one article and one image. This plan does not fund weekly production, parsing experiments or additional model calls. It creates no recurring Codex automation.

### Required acceptance examples

- Brand-document import with traceable fields; setup without a document; repeat import; confirmed-field conflict; visual/text extraction uncertainty; another-project access; revocation across cached/extracted knowledge.
- Two next-week slots prepared once; Monday/weekend publications; first partial week; DST/month crossing; pause/cancel/reschedule; eight monthly units versus nine/ten intended slots.
- New product informs a relevant brief; Wednesday price change affects Thursday's dependent passage; offer expires before publication; variant/currency mismatch; source outage; contradictory catalog/public facts; unchanged articles retained.
- Changed text invalidates old approval; late response and repeated job do not overwrite an owner's edit or recreate a discarded output; shortfall preserves completed work and reports uncovered slots; scheduler cutover/rollback cannot duplicate generation or publication.
- A saved scoped correction affects the next relevant draft; a one-off edit does not rewrite the brand profile; generated claims cannot certify themselves; malicious reference instructions cannot expand tools, spend or data scope.
- Across representative niches/languages, compare factual support, brand compliance, audience usefulness, original value, topic duplication, valid links, visual/product fidelity, owner editing effort and cost per accepted output. Model self-scores alone are insufficient evidence.

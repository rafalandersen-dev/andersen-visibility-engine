# Preserve accounts when confirmation email fails — 12 September 2026

Status: implemented and locally validated on `codex/milo-auth-signup-cleanup-20260912`, unreleased. No PR request, migration, account operation, email or deployment was performed while required security review remains unavailable under the previously reported quota condition.

The signup error handler previously deleted the account returned by `generateLink` when its creation timestamp was less than one minute old. A timestamp does not prove that the current request created that account. A response identifying an existing recent account, a future timestamp or overlapping requests could therefore cause a mail/rendering failure to trigger administrative deletion. This finding is established from source and mocked boundary regressions; no production incident or live Supabase response is claimed.

The handler now checks for absent email configuration before requesting the signup link. After link generation, rendering or delivery errors propagate without deleting the returned account. Successful signup inputs and response, email content and logging, and the generic password-recovery response remain unchanged. The installed Supabase SDK's `GenerateLinkResponse` contains `properties` and `user` metadata, without an explicit marker proving exclusive creation by this request (`@supabase/auth-js/src/lib/types.ts`). The change does not depend on inferring ownership from that metadata.

An unconfirmed account may remain after a later rendering or delivery failure. Preserving it avoids an unjustified destructive cleanup; this change does not claim every retry, confirmation or recovery journey is accepted. Real confirmation/resend/recovery checks remain required, and no automatic email retry, account confirmation or cleanup policy is introduced.

The account-retention change passes all 13. Strengthening the absent-configuration case to require no administrative link call fails before the preflight addition and passes afterward.

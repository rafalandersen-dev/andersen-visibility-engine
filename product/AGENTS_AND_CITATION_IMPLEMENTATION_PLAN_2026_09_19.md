## Evidence and experiments

A verified improvement requires the scoped finding and baseline identity, approved content version and destination receipt. It is countable before retest. A completed proof loop adds a dated comparable retest; defining the first in terms of the second would create a circular acceptance gate.

For the existing small pilot, record: (a) owner-rated usefulness and hands-on minutes; (b) provenance completeness, with any signal conflation a failure; (c) each factual finding's approved correction/receipt or explicit unresolved reason. Proposed operational usefulness threshold: at least 70% of reviewed findings judged actionable and median review/decision time no more than 10 minutes per finding, fixed before collection. These are provisional product targets, not measured results, market superiority, or added launch commitments. Small samples and one owner's judgments must be labelled. No competitor advantage is proven without a matched comparison; buying such access is outside present authorization.

Cost per useful measurement must include relevant acquisition/subscription/storage/execution costs and failed attempts; report unknown amounts explicitly. Owner time stays in minutes unless a labour rate is supplied. Zero model-collection calls does not mean zero total cost. Zero useful results makes unit cost undefined, not zero.

# Language semantics for app and authentication — 12 September 2026

Prepared and locally validated; unreleased. Five `lang` attributes now identify the actual selected interface language on the app shell, mobile navigation sheet, project dropdown menu, authentication form root and password-recovery form root. The two portalled navigation surfaces receive their own attributes because they do not inherit the app wrapper's language in the DOM. Existing `useAppLanguage` and authentication `language` state supply the values; no language persistence, selection, navigation, authentication or recovery behavior is changed.

The root document currently derives its language from the route path. That remains appropriate for fixed market routes and English metadata, but previously made translated app/authentication content inherit English. These scoped attributes identify the translated content without changing root/path/hreflang behavior, reading a prior workspace on auth pages or adding a global DOM mutation. Public page language roots remain the successor's separate scope.
